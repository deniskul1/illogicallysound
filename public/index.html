    Settings

    Bring your own API key. It's stored only in your browser (localStorage) and sent to your own local server, which forwards it straight to your chosen provider, never to us.

    How to play

    The game

    Get a topic and you'll be given a short scenario with a specific person or group proposing something, plus a yes/no question about it. Write an argument responding to that question in 300 characters or fewer, packing in as many logical fallacies as you can while still technically addressing the question.

    Grading

    When you submit, the AI checks your argument against the full canonical list of fallacies (see the reference book icon) and reports every instance it finds, with a quote and explanation for each.

    A few rules it grades strictly by:

    • Your argument has to actually engage with the scenario. A random, unrelated insult with no logical connection to the question doesn't count as a fallacy, it just scores zero.
    • A fallacy like ad hominem only counts if it targets the actual proposer named in the scenario (or a stated supporter), not some unrelated made up person.
    • Each distinct fallacy found is worth 10 points. Repeating the same fallacy twice only counts once.

    Scoring

    The points shown are for your current argument only. They reset with every new topic and don't add up across rounds.
